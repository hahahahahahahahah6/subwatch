# Privacy Policy — subwatch

Last updated: 2026-10-01

subwatch is a Chrome extension that notifies you about new posts in
subreddits you choose to monitor.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** subwatch does not collect, transmit, or share any personal
data.

- Your monitored subreddits and keywords are stored **only** in your
  browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). They never
  leave your device.
- The extension periodically fetches **public** subreddit listing pages
  from old.reddit.com to detect new posts. No login, no account, no
  cookies are sent beyond what your browser normally sends when
  visiting those public pages.
- No analytics, no tracking, no third-party services.

## Permissions used

- `storage` — saves your watchlist locally.
- `notifications` — shows desktop alerts for new posts.
- `alarms` — schedules periodic checks.
- Host access to `old.reddit.com` — reads public subreddit listings.
